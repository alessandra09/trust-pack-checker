# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") between the customer named in the order form (the "Customer") and Northwind Voice Ltd, a company registered in Ireland ("Northwind"). It applies where Northwind processes Customer Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in providing its cloud call recording and transcription services (the "Services").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Customer Personal Data" means personal data that Northwind processes on behalf of the Customer under the Agreement. "GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Terms such as "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data breach" and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given in the GDPR.

1.2 "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by Northwind to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles and scope

2.1 The Customer is the controller and Northwind is the processor of Customer Personal Data. The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex 1, which also sets out the obligations and rights of the Customer as controller.

2.2 This DPA remains in force for as long as Northwind processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

## 3. Instructions

3.1 Northwind shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organisation, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which Northwind is subject. In such a case, Northwind shall inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

3.2 The Agreement, this DPA and the Customer's configuration of the Services are the Customer's complete documented instructions at the date of signature. Further instructions must be given in writing.

3.3 Northwind shall immediately inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection provisions.

## 4. Confidentiality

4.1 Northwind shall ensure that all persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality, and that access is limited to personnel who need it to provide the Services.

## 5. Security

5.1 Northwind shall take all measures required pursuant to Article 32 GDPR. Taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation and the nature, scope, context and purposes of processing, Northwind shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including the measures described in Annex 2.

## 6. Sub-processors

6.1 The Customer gives Northwind general written authorisation to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex 3. Northwind shall not engage another processor without this prior general written authorisation of the Customer.

6.2 Northwind shall inform the Customer of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of Sub-processors by email at least thirty (30) days in advance, thereby giving the Customer the opportunity to object to such changes. If the Customer objects on reasonable data protection grounds, the parties shall discuss the objection in good faith, and the Customer may terminate the affected Services.

6.3 Where Northwind engages a Sub-processor, it shall impose on that Sub-processor, by way of a written contract, the same data protection obligations as set out in this DPA, in particular providing sufficient guarantees to implement appropriate technical and organisational measures. Where a Sub-processor fails to fulfil its data protection obligations, Northwind shall remain fully liable to the Customer for the performance of that Sub-processor's obligations.

6.4 Northwind shall respect the conditions set out in this Section 6 and in Article 28(2) and 28(4) GDPR when it engages another processor.

## 7. International transfers

7.1 Northwind processes Customer Personal Data in the European Economic Area. Where a Sub-processor processes Customer Personal Data in a third country, Northwind shall ensure that the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision or by the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission (Decision (EU) 2021/914), Module Three, which the parties agree to apply to such transfers.

## 8. Assistance with data subject rights

8.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, Northwind shall assist the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, for the fulfilment of the Customer's obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights laid down in Chapter III of the GDPR.

8.2 If Northwind receives a request directly from a data subject, it shall forward the request to the Customer within five (5) business days and shall not respond to it except on the Customer's instructions.

## 9. Personal data breaches and further assistance

9.1 Northwind shall notify the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data. The notice shall describe the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences and the measures taken or proposed.

9.2 Taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to Northwind, Northwind shall assist the Customer in ensuring compliance with its obligations pursuant to Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, including security of processing, notification of personal data breaches to the supervisory authority and to data subjects, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with the supervisory authority.

## 10. Deletion or return of personal data

10.1 At the choice of the Customer, Northwind shall delete or return all Customer Personal Data to the Customer after the end of the provision of the Services, and shall delete existing copies unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the personal data. Deletion, including from backups, shall be completed within thirty (30) days after the end of the Services.

## 11. Information and audits

11.1 Northwind shall make available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in Article 28 GDPR and this DPA, and shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Customer or another auditor mandated by the Customer. Audits shall take place on at least thirty (30) days' written notice, during business hours, and no more than once a year unless a personal data breach has occurred or a supervisory authority requires it.

## 12. Use of data for artificial intelligence

12.1 Northwind shall not use Customer Personal Data, including call recordings and transcripts, to train, fine-tune or improve any machine learning or artificial intelligence model, whether its own or a third party's.

## 13. Liability and contacts

13.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitation of liability in the Agreement.

13.2 Northwind's data protection officer can be contacted at dpo@northwind-voice.example.

## Annex 1: Details of processing

Subject matter: provision of cloud call recording, transcription and search.
Duration: the term of the Agreement plus the deletion period in Section 10.
Nature and purpose of the processing: recording, storing, transcribing and indexing business phone calls so that the Customer can review them for quality and dispute resolution.
Types of personal data: names, phone numbers, call recordings, transcripts, call metadata.
Categories of data subjects: the Customer's employees and the people they speak with on the phone.
Obligations and rights of the controller: as set out in the Agreement and this DPA.

## Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

- Encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest using AES-256.
- Multi-factor authentication for all staff access to production systems; role-based access with least privilege.
- Central logging of administrative access, retained for twelve months.
- Daily encrypted backups stored in a second EEA region.
- Annual penetration test by an independent firm.
