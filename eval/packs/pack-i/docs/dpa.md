# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This agreement is concluded between the merchant using the payment service (the "Controller") and Larkspur Payments Oy, Helsinki, Finland (the "Processor").

## Article 1. Subject matter

1.1 The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller to provide card payment processing and fraud screening.

1.2 Appendix A describes the subject matter and duration of the processing, the nature and purpose of the processing, the type of personal data, the categories of data subjects and the Controller's obligations and rights.

## Article 2. Instructions

2.1 The Processor processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless Union or Member State law requires otherwise, in which case it informs the Controller of that requirement before processing unless the law prohibits this.

2.2 If the Processor considers that any instruction of the Controller infringes the GDPR, it shall promptly inform the Controller.

## Article 3. Personnel

3.1 Processor personnel who handle payment data complete annual privacy and security training.

## Article 4. Security

4.1 The Processor implements the technical and organisational measures listed in Appendix B to meet Article 32 GDPR.

## Article 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Controller gives general written authorisation for the sub-processors listed in Appendix C. The Processor informs the Controller of intended changes at least fourteen (14) days in advance, and the Controller may object.

5.2 The Processor engages sub-processors only under the conditions of this Article and imposes the same data protection obligations on them by written contract. The Processor remains fully liable for its sub-processors.

## Article 6. Assistance

6.1 The Processor assists the Controller in ensuring compliance with Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, including data protection impact assessments and prior consultation.

## Article 7. Breaches

7.1 The Processor notifies the Controller of a personal data breach without undue delay and within twenty-four (24) hours.

## Article 8. Audits

8.1 The Processor makes available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR and allows for and contributes to audits, including inspections, by the Controller or an auditor mandated by it.

## Appendix A: Processing details

Subject matter: card payment processing and fraud screening.
Duration: the term of the merchant agreement.
Nature and purpose: authorising card payments and screening them for fraud.
Type of personal data: cardholder name, masked card number, transaction data, IP address.
Categories of data subjects: the Controller's customers who pay by card.

## Appendix B: Security measures

Payment data is encrypted in transit and at rest; card numbers are tokenised.
