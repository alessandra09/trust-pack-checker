# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Data Processing Addendum ("Addendum") forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement between Kestrel Analytics, Inc. ("Service Provider") and Moorfield Dental Group Ltd ("Customer").

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Data Protection Law" means the GDPR, the UK GDPR and any other law on the processing of personal data that applies to the Services.

1.2 "Customer Personal Data" means personal data that Service Provider processes on behalf of Customer under the Agreement.

1.3 "Personal Data Breach" has the meaning given in the GDPR.

1.4 "Subcontractor" means any third party that Service Provider engages to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 Schedule 1 sets out the subject matter and duration of the processing, the nature and purpose of the processing, the types of Customer Personal Data and the categories of data subjects.

2.2 Customer is the controller and Service Provider is the processor of Customer Personal Data.

## 3. CUSTOMER INSTRUCTIONS

3.1 Service Provider shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from Customer, including with regard to transfers to a third country, unless the law to which Service Provider is subject requires otherwise. In that case Service Provider shall inform Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless the law prohibits this.

3.2 The Agreement, this Addendum and Customer's use of the Services' settings are Customer's documented instructions.

3.3 Service Provider shall promptly notify Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

## 4. SECURITY

4.1 Service Provider shall implement and maintain the technical and organizational measures described in Schedule 2. Service Provider may update these measures, provided the overall level of protection is not reduced.

## 5. PERSONAL DATA BREACHES

5.1 Service Provider shall notify Customer without undue delay, and in any case within 36 hours, after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data.

5.2 The notice will describe the nature of the Personal Data Breach, the likely consequences and the steps Service Provider has taken.

## 6. SUBCONTRACTORS

6.1 Customer grants Service Provider a general authorization to engage the Subcontractors listed on the Subcontractor Page at the date of this Addendum.

6.2 Service Provider shall give Customer at least 14 days' notice by email of any new Subcontractor. Customer may object on reasonable grounds within that period, and the parties will work together in good faith to address the objection.

6.3 Service Provider shall engage a Subcontractor only in accordance with this Section 6.

6.4 Each Subcontractor must be bound by a written contract with data protection terms that protect Customer Personal Data to at least the standard of this Addendum. Service Provider remains responsible to Customer for the acts and omissions of each Subcontractor.

## 7. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS

7.1 Service Provider shall assist Customer, by appropriate technical and organizational measures, in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Data Protection Law.

7.2 If a data subject contacts Service Provider directly, Service Provider shall pass the request to Customer and shall not respond except on Customer's instructions.

## 8. AUDITS

8.1 Service Provider shall make available to Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 of the GDPR.

8.2 Service Provider shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Customer or an independent auditor mandated by Customer, on 30 days' written notice and not more than once in any 12-month period.

## 9. TRANSFERS

9.1 Service Provider processes Customer Personal Data in the United States. Transfers from the EEA and the United Kingdom are made under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (module two) and the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, which are incorporated into this Addendum.

## 10. TERM

10.1 This Addendum stays in force for as long as Service Provider processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

## SCHEDULE 1. DETAILS OF PROCESSING

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Subject matter | Appointment and billing analytics for Customer's dental clinics |
| Duration | The term of the Agreement |
| Nature and purpose | Hosting, analysis and reporting on appointment data |
| Types of data | Name, contact details, appointment history, billing amounts |
| Categories of data subjects | Customer's patients and staff |

## SCHEDULE 2. TECHNICAL AND ORGANIZATIONAL MEASURES

- Encryption of Customer Personal Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256).
- Single sign-on with multi-factor authentication for all administrative access.
- Role-based access, reviewed every quarter.
- Daily backups kept for 30 days in a separate region.
- Annual penetration test by an independent firm.
