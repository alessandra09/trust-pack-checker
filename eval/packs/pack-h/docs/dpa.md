# CUSTOMER DATA PROCESSING TERMS

These terms apply between Orrin Telematics B.V., Rotterdam, the Netherlands ("Orrin"), and the fleet operator that subscribes to Orrin's tracking service ("Customer").

## 1. Roles

1.1 Orrin acts as processor of the driver and vehicle data that the Customer, as controller, places in the service ("Customer Data").

## 2. Security

2.1 Customer Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest.

## 3. Incidents

3.1 Orrin informs the Customer of a personal data breach within thirty-six (36) hours of detecting it.

## 4. Sub-processors

4.1 Orrin engages these sub-processors:

• Helix Maps GmbH – map rendering – Germany
• Sable Compute Inc. – GPU processing of route data – United States
• Tarn Messaging AB – push notifications – Sweden

4.2 Orrin gives thirty (30) days' notice of new sub-processors.

## 5. End of contract

5.1 Customer Data is purged no later than forty-five (45) days after the contract ends.

5.2 Backups are kept for thirty (30) days.

## 6. Audits

6.1 The Customer may audit Orrin's processing on-site once per year with thirty (30) days' notice.

## 7. Models

7.1 Orrin will not use Customer Data for training machine learning models.

## 8. Contact

8.1 Data protection officer: dpo@orrin.example.
