# DATA PROCESSING TERMS

These Data Processing Terms ("Terms") are made between Fjordline Analytics ApS, a company registered in Denmark (the "Supplier"), and the client named in the order (the "Client"). They govern the Supplier's processing of personal data on the Client's behalf when providing web analytics dashboards (the "Service").

## 1. Scope

(a) For the purposes of the GDPR, the Client is the controller and the Supplier is the processor.

(b) Schedule 1 sets out the subject matter and duration of the processing, the nature and purpose of the processing, the type of personal data and the categories of data subjects, and the Client's obligations and rights.

## 2. Acting on the Client's instructions

(a) The Supplier will process the personal data only on documented instructions from the Client, including in relation to transfers of personal data to a third country, unless Union or Member State law requires otherwise; in that case the Supplier will tell the Client of that legal requirement first, unless the law forbids this on important grounds of public interest.

(b) If the Supplier believes that an instruction from the Client infringes the GDPR or other data protection law, it will tell the Client immediately.

## 3. Security

(a) The Supplier will implement the technical and organisational measures described in Schedule 2, which are designed to meet the requirements of Article 32 GDPR.

## 4. Other processors

(a) The Client grants the Supplier a general written authorisation to use the sub-processors listed in Schedule 3.

(b) The Supplier will give the Client at least twenty (20) days' notice of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of a sub-processor, and the Client may object to such changes on reasonable grounds.

(c) When the Supplier engages a sub-processor, it will do so only under the conditions in this Section 4 and will impose the same data protection obligations as in these Terms by written contract. The Supplier remains fully liable to the Client for each sub-processor.

## 5. Transfers

(a) Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA, the Supplier will rely on an adequacy decision or the Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 6. Personal data breaches

(a) The Supplier will inform the Client of any personal data breach without undue delay and in any case within forty-eight (48) hours of discovering it.

## 7. End of the Service

(a) On termination, the Supplier will, at the Client's choice, return or delete all personal data and delete existing copies within sixty (60) days, unless the law requires the Supplier to keep them.

## 8. Premises

(a) The Supplier's offices are in a shared building; the fire-safety systems of the building are subject to an annual inspection by the building owner.

## 9. Contacts

(a) Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@fjordline.example.

## Schedule 1: Processing details

Subject matter: hosting and reporting of website visit analytics.
Duration: the term of the Client's subscription and the period in Section 7.
Nature and purpose: collecting, aggregating and displaying website visit statistics for the Client.
Type of personal data: IP addresses (truncated), device identifiers, pages visited.
Categories of data subjects: visitors to the Client's websites.

## Schedule 2: Security measures

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Production access uses single sign-on with multi-factor authentication. Supplier personnel access to production systems is logged and reviewed every quarter.

## Schedule 3: Sub-processors

- Nordlys Cloud AS, infrastructure hosting, Norway
- Pellucid Mail Ltd, email delivery, Ireland
