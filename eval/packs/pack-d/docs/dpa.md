# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") is entered into between the customer identified in the order form (the "Customer") and Harbourline Payroll Ltd, a company registered in Ireland ("Harbourline"). It applies where Harbourline processes Customer Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in providing its hosted payroll and expense services (the "Services").

## 1. Roles and scope

1.1 The Customer is the controller and Harbourline is the processor of Customer Personal Data. The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex 1, which also sets out the obligations and rights of the Customer.

## 2. Instructions

2.1 Harbourline shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or an international organisation, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which Harbourline is subject; in such a case Harbourline shall inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

2.2 Harbourline shall immediately inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection provisions.

## 3. Confidentiality

3.1 Harbourline shall ensure that persons authorised to process Customer Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

## 4. Security

4.1 Harbourline shall take all measures required pursuant to Article 32 GDPR, including the technical and organisational measures described in Annex 2.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Customer gives Harbourline general written authorisation to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex 3.

5.2 Harbourline shall inform the Customer by email of any intended addition or replacement of a Sub-processor at least thirty (30) days before the change, giving the Customer the opportunity to object to it.

5.3 Harbourline shall impose on each Sub-processor, by way of a written contract, the same data protection obligations as set out in this DPA. Harbourline remains fully liable to the Customer for the performance of each Sub-processor's obligations.

## 6. International transfers

6.1 Where a Sub-processor processes Customer Personal Data outside the European Economic Area, Harbourline shall ensure that the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision or by the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission.

## 7. Assistance

7.1 Taking into account the nature of the processing, Harbourline shall assist the Customer by appropriate technical and organisational measures in fulfilling its obligation to respond to requests for exercising the data subject's rights.

7.2 Harbourline shall assist the Customer in ensuring compliance with its obligations under Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, including data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with the supervisory authority.

## 8. Personal data breaches

8.1 Harbourline shall notify the Customer of a Personal Data Breach without undue delay and in any event within seventy-two (72) hours after becoming aware of it, and shall provide the information the Customer needs to meet its own notification duties.

## 9. Deletion and return

9.1 Within ninety (90) days after termination of the Agreement, Harbourline shall, at the Customer's choice, delete or return all Customer Personal Data and delete existing copies, unless Union or Member State law requires storage of the personal data.

9.2 The Customer may export Customer Personal Data at any time during the term and for fourteen (14) days after termination.

9.3 Backup copies containing Customer Personal Data are overwritten within thirty-five (35) days.

## 10. Audits

10.1 Harbourline shall make available to the Customer all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR. The Customer's audit rights are limited to reviewing Harbourline's most recent independent SOC 2 Type II report, which Harbourline shall provide on request once per year.

## 11. Product improvement

11.1 Harbourline may use Customer Personal Data in de-identified form to train and improve its machine learning models for expense categorisation.

## 12. Contact

12.1 Harbourline's data protection officer can be contacted at dpo@harbourline.example.

## Annex 1: Details of processing

Subject matter: hosted payroll calculation and expense management.
Duration: the term of the Agreement plus the deletion period in Section 9.
Nature and purpose of the processing: calculating salaries, producing payslips and processing employee expense claims for the Customer.
Types of personal data: names, employee numbers, bank account details, salary data, expense receipts.
Categories of data subjects: the Customer's employees and contractors.

## Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

Customer Personal Data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher and encrypted at rest using AES-256. Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication and is granted on a least-privilege basis. Harbourline keeps a written incident response plan and tests its business continuity plan once a year.
