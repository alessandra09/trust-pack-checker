# Processing Terms

Between Calloway Outdoor Ltd (the "Client") and Thornbury Freight Systems A/S (the "Supplier"). These Processing Terms form part of the Freight Platform Agreement and apply whenever the Supplier handles personal data for the Client.

## Part I. What the Supplier does

(a) The Supplier runs a shipment tracking platform. To do so it handles names, delivery addresses, telephone numbers and delivery notes of the Client's customers, together with the names and work email addresses of the Client's warehouse staff ("Client Data").

(b) Annex 1 sets out the subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of Client Data and the categories of individuals concerned, as well as the obligations and rights of the Client.

(c) For the purposes of the GDPR the Client is the controller and the Supplier is the processor.

## Part II. Instructions and people

(a) The Supplier will not process Client Data for any purpose other than on the Client's documented instructions, including instructions about transfers of Client Data to a third country, unless the law of the Union or of a Member State requires otherwise; in that case the Supplier will tell the Client about that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits this on important grounds of public interest.

(b) The Client's instructions are these Processing Terms, the settings the Client chooses in the platform, and any further written instructions from the Client's operations manager.

(c) No member of the Supplier's staff may access Client Data unless that person is bound by a duty of confidentiality, either by contract or by law. Access is limited to the staff who need it to run and support the platform.

## Part III. Subcontracted processors

(a) The Client gives the Supplier a general written authorisation to use subcontracted processors. The current list is in Annex 3.

(b) The Supplier will tell the Client by email at least 30 days before it adds or replaces a subcontracted processor. The Client may object within that period on reasonable data protection grounds, and the parties will then discuss the change in good faith.

(c) The Supplier will engage a subcontracted processor only under the conditions set out in this Part III.

## Part IV. Security

(a) The Supplier will put in place and maintain appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect Client Data against accidental destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure or access. Annex 2 describes these measures.

(b) The Supplier will tell the Client without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after it becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting Client Data.

## Part V. Requests from individuals

(a) Taking into account the nature of the processing, the Supplier will, by appropriate technical and organisational measures and as far as possible, help the Client to respond to requests from individuals who exercise their rights under Chapter III GDPR.

(b) If an individual contacts the Supplier directly, the Supplier will pass the request to the Client within five working days and will not answer it itself.

## Part VI. Checks by the Client

(a) The Supplier will make available to the Client all information necessary to demonstrate that it meets the obligations in Article 28 GDPR.

(b) The Supplier will also allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by the Client or by an auditor the Client appoints. The Client will give 30 days' notice and will not audit more than once a year unless a personal data breach has occurred.

## Part VII. International transfers

(a) The Supplier and its subcontracted processors keep Client Data in the European Economic Area. Support staff of a subcontracted processor in the United Kingdom may view Client Data to resolve support tickets, on the basis of the adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

## Part VIII. General

(a) These Processing Terms last as long as the Supplier handles Client Data under the Freight Platform Agreement.

(b) Danish law governs these Processing Terms.

## Annex 1. Details of the processing

- Subject matter: shipment tracking and delivery notifications.
- Duration: the term of the Freight Platform Agreement.
- Nature and purpose: storing, displaying and sending delivery updates.
- Types of Client Data: names, delivery addresses, telephone numbers, delivery notes, staff work email addresses.
- Individuals: the Client's customers and warehouse staff.

## Annex 2. Security measures and end of the services

- Encryption of Client Data in transit (TLS 1.2 or higher) and at rest (AES-256).
- Single sign-on with multi-factor authentication for all staff accounts.
- Daily encrypted backups kept for 21 days.
- Yearly penetration test by an external firm.

When the Freight Platform Agreement ends, the Supplier will, at the Client's choice, delete or return all Client Data and delete existing copies within 45 days, unless Union or Member State law requires the Supplier to keep them. Backups are overwritten in the normal 21-day cycle.
