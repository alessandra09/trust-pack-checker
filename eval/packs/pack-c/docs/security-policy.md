# NORTHWIND VOICE INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY

Version 3.2. Owner: Head of Security. This policy applies to all Northwind Voice Ltd staff, contractors and systems that store or process customer data.

## 1. Governance

1.1 The Head of Security owns this policy and reviews it every year. The executive team approves changes. Security risks are tracked in a risk register that is reviewed every quarter.

1.2 All staff complete security awareness training when they join and every year after that. Training covers phishing, password hygiene, handling customer data and reporting incidents.

## 2. Access control

2.1 Access to production systems requires multi-factor authentication. Access is granted on a least privilege basis according to role, approved by the system owner and reviewed every quarter.

2.2 Accounts of leavers are disabled on their last working day. Shared accounts are not allowed.

## 3. Encryption

3.1 Customer data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Customer data at rest, including call recordings and backups, is encrypted using AES-256. Encryption keys are managed in a cloud key management service and rotated every year.

## 4. Logging and monitoring

4.1 Administrative access and security events are logged centrally. Logs are retained for ninety (90) days and monitored for suspicious activity by an on-call engineer.

## 5. Backup and disaster recovery

5.1 Customer data is backed up daily to a second EEA region. Backups are retained for thirty-five (35) days and then overwritten.

5.2 The disaster recovery plan is tested every year. The recovery time objective is eight hours.

## 6. Vulnerability management

6.1 Production systems are scanned for vulnerabilities every week. Critical vulnerabilities are patched within seven (7) days of discovery.

6.2 An independent firm performs penetration tests at least every six (6) months, and findings are tracked to closure.

## 7. Incident response

7.1 Northwind Voice maintains an incident response plan with defined roles, severity levels and communication templates. The plan is rehearsed every year.

7.2 If a security incident affects customer personal data, Northwind Voice will notify affected customers within twenty-four (24) hours of confirming the incident.

## 8. Suppliers

8.1 New suppliers that will access customer data go through a security review before contracts are signed. Changes to suppliers that process customer data are announced to customers fourteen (14) days in advance.

## 9. Physical security

9.1 Northwind Voice does not operate its own data centres. Physical security of hosting facilities is the responsibility of the hosting provider.
