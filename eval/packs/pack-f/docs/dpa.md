# DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Addendum is between Quillon Signals Ltd, registered in England and Wales ("Quillon"), and the customer that accepted Quillon's terms of service ("Customer"). Quillon processes personal data for the Customer to send and receive SMS messages through its platform.

## 1. Roles

1.1 The Customer is the controller and Quillon is the processor.

## 2. Instructions and confidentiality

2.1 Quillon processes Customer personal data only on the Customer's documented instructions, including for transfers to third countries, unless EU or Member State law requires otherwise.

2.2 Quillon ensures that its staff with access to Customer personal data are bound by confidentiality.

## 3. Sub-processors

3.1 The Customer authorises Quillon to use the following sub-processors:

- Amberline Hosting GmbH: cloud infrastructure, Germany
- Corvid Telecom Ltd: carrier connections, Ireland
- Tessel Support Pvt Ltd: first-line customer support, India

3.2 Quillon will notify the Customer at least thirty (30) days before adding a new sub-processor, and the Customer may object.

## 4. Location of processing

4.1 Message data is stored in Germany. First-line support staff of Tessel Support Pvt Ltd may view message metadata from India under the Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. Security

5.1 Quillon applies the measures required by Article 32 GDPR, including encryption of data in transit and at rest.

## 6. Incidents

6.1 Quillon will notify the Customer of a personal data breach without undue delay and no later than two (2) business days after becoming aware of it.

## 7. Retention and deletion

7.1 After the account is closed, Quillon deletes Customer personal data within thirty (30) calendar days.

7.2 Message logs are deleted after thirty (30) days.

## 8. Audits

8.1 No on-site audits will be permitted. Instead, Quillon will provide its current ISO 27001 certificate on request.

## 9. Use of data

9.1 Quillon may use message content to train its spam-detection models.

## 10. Contact

10.1 Quillon's data protection officer: dpo@quillon.example.
