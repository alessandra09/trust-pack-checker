# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

Between Brisa HR Software S.L., Madrid, Spain ("Brisa"), and the customer named in the subscription ("Customer").

## 1. Subject

1.1 Brisa processes the Customer's employee data as processor when providing its HR and leave-management software.

## 2. Security

2.1 Brisa encrypts Customer Personal Data in transit and at rest and holds a SOC 2 Type II report covering the service.

## 3. Personal data breaches

3.1 Brisa shall notify the Customer of any personal data breach without undue delay after becoming aware of it.

## 4. Sub-processors

4.1 Brisa uses the following sub-processors, which the Customer approves by signing this Agreement:

| Name                     | Service         | Country       |
| ------------------------ | --------------- | ------------- |
| Solana Datacenters S.A.  | Hosting         | Spain         |
| Ridgefield Helpdesk Inc. | Support tickets | United States |

4.2 Support tickets are handled by Ridgefield Helpdesk Inc. in the United States.

## 5. Backups and deletion

5.1 Backups are retained for up to six (6) months and then overwritten.

5.2 Brisa deletes Customer Personal Data within thirty (30) days after the end of the subscription.

## 6. Audits

6.1 Audit rights are satisfied by Brisa providing its SOC 2 Type II report once a year.

## 7. Artificial intelligence

7.1 Brisa shall not use Customer Personal Data to train artificial intelligence models.
