# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") between the customer named in the order form (the "Customer") and Northwind Voice Ltd, a company registered in Ireland ("Northwind"). It applies where Northwind processes Customer Personal Data on behalf of the Customer in providing its cloud call recording and transcription services (the "Services").

## 1. Definitions

1.1 "Customer Personal Data" means personal data that Northwind processes on behalf of the Customer under the Agreement. "GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679. Terms such as "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data breach" and "supervisory authority" have the meanings given in the GDPR.

1.2 "Sub-processor" means any third party engaged by Northwind to process Customer Personal Data.

## 2. Roles and scope

2.1 The Customer is the controller and Northwind is the processor of Customer Personal Data. The subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of the processing, the types of personal data and the categories of data subjects are described in Annex 1, which also sets out the obligations and rights of the Customer as controller.

2.2 This DPA remains in force for as long as Northwind processes Customer Personal Data under the Agreement.

## 3. Instructions

3.1 Northwind shall process Customer Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, unless required to do so by Union or Member State law to which Northwind is subject. In such a case, Northwind shall inform the Customer of that legal requirement before processing, unless that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

3.2 The Agreement, this DPA and the Customer's configuration of the Services are the Customer's complete documented instructions at the date of signature. Further instructions must be given in writing.

3.3 Northwind shall immediately inform the Customer if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes the GDPR or other Union or Member State data protection provisions.

## 4. Security

4.1 Northwind shall take all measures required pursuant to Article 32 GDPR, including the technical and organisational measures described in Annex 2, which is incorporated by reference and available at https://northwind-voice.example/security-measures.

## 5. Sub-processors

5.1 The Customer gives Northwind general written authorisation to engage the Sub-processors listed in Annex 3. Northwind shall not engage another processor without this prior general written authorisation of the Customer.

5.2 Where Northwind engages a Sub-processor, it shall impose on that Sub-processor, by way of a written contract, the same data protection obligations as set out in this DPA, in particular providing sufficient guarantees to implement appropriate technical and organisational measures. Where a Sub-processor fails to fulfil its data protection obligations, Northwind shall remain fully liable to the Customer for the performance of that Sub-processor's obligations.

5.3 Northwind shall respect the conditions set out in this Section 5 and in Article 28(2) and 28(4) GDPR when it engages another processor.

## 6. International transfers

6.1 Northwind processes Customer Personal Data in the European Economic Area. Where a Sub-processor processes Customer Personal Data in a third country, Northwind shall ensure that the transfer is covered by an adequacy decision or by the Standard Contractual Clauses adopted by the European Commission (Decision (EU) 2021/914), Module Three, which the parties agree to apply to such transfers.

## 7. Personal data breaches and further assistance

7.1 Northwind shall notify the Customer of a personal data breach affecting Customer Personal Data within a reasonable time after becoming aware of it.

7.2 Taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to Northwind, Northwind shall assist the Customer in ensuring compliance with its obligations pursuant to Articles 32 to 36 GDPR, including security of processing, notification of personal data breaches to the supervisory authority, data protection impact assessments and prior consultation with the supervisory authority.

## 8. Backups after the Agreement ends

8.1 Northwind keeps Customer Personal Data in its backups for thirty (30) days after the Agreement ends.

## 9. Use of data for artificial intelligence

9.1 Northwind shall not use Customer Personal Data, including call recordings and transcripts, to train, fine-tune or improve any machine learning or artificial intelligence model, whether its own or a third party's.

## 10. Liability, contacts and corporate information

10.1 Each party's liability under this DPA is subject to the limitation of liability in the Agreement.

10.2 Northwind's data protection officer can be contacted at dpo@northwind-voice.example.

10.3 Northwind's annual financial statements are subject to a statutory audit by an independent accounting firm, and its audited accounts are filed each year with the Companies Registration Office.

## Annex 1: Details of processing

Subject matter: provision of cloud call recording, transcription and search.
Duration: the term of the Agreement. Backups of Customer Personal Data are kept for ninety (90) days after the Agreement ends.
Nature and purpose of the processing: recording, storing, transcribing and indexing business phone calls so that the Customer can review them for quality and dispute resolution, and improving Northwind's speech recognition models by training them on call recordings.
Types of personal data: names, phone numbers, call recordings, transcripts, call metadata.
Categories of data subjects: the Customer's employees and the people they speak with on the phone.
Obligations and rights of the controller: as set out in the Agreement and this DPA.
