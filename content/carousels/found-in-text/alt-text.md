# Alt text: found-in-text

1. Cover slide with the title "Found in text: why every answer needs proof" and space for a photo of the author.
2. Slide titled "The problem": A questionnaire answer says "Yes". Nobody can tell which document says so, or whether it still does.
3. Slide titled ""Found in text" means a quote": The exact sentence from your own document, with the document name and page. No quote, no "found".
4. Slide titled ""Not found in text" is useful too": It shows what your documents do not cover yet. That is your list of gaps to fix before the customer finds them.
5. Slide titled "Numbers are compared by code": Answer: "deleted within 30 days". Contract: "within 45 days". A mismatch like this is flagged with both quotes side by side.
6. Checklist slide titled "Save this: before you send an answer": Quote the sentence it is based on; Name the document and page; Check numbers and durations match; Mark what is not covered; Have a person review it.
7. Final slide: "Try the free checker, link in bio". It runs in your browser: your documents stay on your machine. Every result comes with the quote and page it is based on. Draft for human review, not legal advice.
