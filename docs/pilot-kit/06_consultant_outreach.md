# First message to a DPO consultant

For LinkedIn (under 300 characters for a connection note, longer for a message) or email. Replace the bracketed parts. Send by hand; do not automate LinkedIn messages.

## Version A: direct offer

> Hi [name], I built a free browser tool that checks a DPA against GDPR Article 28 and shows the quote and page behind every finding. Could I run it on one anonymised DPA of yours and send you the result? I'd value your feedback.

**Critique:** clear and short, but it opens with "I built", which is about me, and gives no reason why a consultant should care. No context on who I am.

## Version B: problem first

> Hi [name], DPA reviews for clients often start with the same first pass: is every Article 28 element there, and where. I made a tool that does that pass in the browser, with quotes and pages. Would one free trial review on an anonymised DPA be useful to you?

**Critique:** leads with their work, which is better. Still no credibility line, and "often" is an assumption I have not tested with them.

## Version C: context, then a small ask

> Hi [name], my role grew from billing reconciliation into leading GDPR compliance at a US telecom, and I built Trust Pack Checker to make the first pass on a DPA faster: every Article 28 finding comes with the exact quote and page. It runs in the browser, so documents stay on your machine. Could I review one anonymised DPA for you for free, in exchange for 15 minutes of honest feedback?

**Critique:** gives context, the benefit and a small, concrete ask. A bit long for a connection note; fine for a message or email.

## Best final

> Hi [name], my role grew from billing reconciliation into leading GDPR compliance, and I built Trust Pack Checker to speed up the first pass on a DPA: every Article 28 finding shows the exact quote and page, and documents stay in your browser. Would you like a free review of one anonymised client DPA, in exchange for 15 minutes of feedback? Sample report: https://alessandra09.github.io/trust-pack-checker/sample-report/
