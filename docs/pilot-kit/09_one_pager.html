A faster first pass on a DPA

Trust Pack Checker, for DPO consultants and privacy firms who review processor agreements for clients.

What it does

  • Checks a DPA against the GDPR Article 28 elements in its rule list.
  • Shows the exact quote and page behind every finding, or says "not found in text".
  • Compares a vendor's questionnaire answers with its DPA: breach notice periods, sub-processors, EU/EEA location claims, audit rights, AI training.
  • Drafts questions to send the other side.
  • Runs in the browser: documents stay on your machine.

Early results on synthetic packs the checks had not seen

3/3missing clauses found
3/6vendor contradictions found
4/4clean vendor answers left alone

Small synthetic samples, checked without AI (measured 2026-11-26). Every miss is published on the site.

Free pilot: send one anonymised DPA and get a short report within 3 working days, in exchange for 15 minutes of feedback. Personal data and confidential commercial terms removed first; I delete the file after the review.
