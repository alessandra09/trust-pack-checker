# First message to a founder of a small B2B SaaS company

Use when the founder has just mentioned a customer security questionnaire or a DPA request (a post, a job ad for compliance, a procurement delay). Send by hand.

## Version A: direct offer

> Hi [name], I saw you're working through customer security reviews. I built a free tool that drafts questionnaire answers from your own documents, with a quote for each answer. Want me to try it on yours?

**Critique:** relevant trigger, but "drafts answers" may sound like the tool answers for them. It does not say what happens to their documents.

## Version B: their deadline first

> Hi [name], a customer questionnaire with a deadline is a week of chasing people for answers. Trust Pack Checker maps each question to your own DPA and policies and shows the quote behind every draft answer, or marks it as a gap. Would a free first pass on one questionnaire help?

**Critique:** speaks to the pain and explains gaps honestly. "A week" is a guess I can't back. No line on privacy, which founders will ask about.

## Version C: context and privacy

> Hi [name], my role grew from billing reconciliation into leading GDPR compliance. I built Trust Pack Checker: it drafts answers only from your own documents, shows the quote behind each one, and marks what your documents don't cover. It runs in the browser. Could I do a free first pass on one questionnaire (up to 50 questions) in exchange for feedback?

**Critique:** credible, honest about gaps, mentions privacy and the limit. Slightly long but each line earns its place.

## Best final

> Hi [name], my role grew from billing reconciliation into leading GDPR compliance, and I built Trust Pack Checker to help with customer security questionnaires: it drafts answers only from your own documents, shows the quote behind each one, and marks the gaps. Could I do a free first pass on one questionnaire (up to 50 questions, personal data and confidential terms removed) in exchange for 15 minutes of feedback?
