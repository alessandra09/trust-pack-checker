# Feedback questions (15 minutes)

1. How long does a review like this take you today, and who does it?
2. What do you check first when a DPA or questionnaire arrives?
3. What was missing from this report, or what would you not trust in it?
4. Would you pay for this, and how much would feel fair per document or per month?

**Testimonial permission:** "May I quote what you said, with your name and company, or anonymously as '[role] at a [type of company]'? You will see the exact text before I use it."
